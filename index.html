About Chico, CA

Chico is a vibrant Northern California city known for its tree-lined streets, California State University, and strong community spirit. Situated in Butte County, it offers an affordable alternative to Bay Area prices with a high quality of life.

  • Home to CSU Chico & a thriving arts scene
  • Bidwell Park — one of the largest city-owned parks in the US
  • Strong local dining, breweries & farmers markets
  • Easy access to Sacramento & the Sierra Nevada

Chico Market Snapshot

The Chico real estate market remains active with steady demand from both local buyers and those relocating from higher-cost California metros. Inventory has been tight in recent years, making experienced guidance essential.

  • Median home price: ~$430,000–$480,000
  • Strong demand in historic Avenues neighborhoods
  • Growth in outlying communities like Forest Ranch
  • Julie has served Chico buyers & sellers for 10+ years

About the Tri-Valley

The Tri-Valley — spanning Dublin, Pleasanton, Livermore, San Ramon, and Danville — is one of the most sought-after regions in the Bay Area. Known for top-rated schools, family-friendly communities, and more space than the peninsula.

  • Top-ranked public schools in California
  • BART access to San Francisco & Oakland
  • Livermore Valley wine country
  • Tech corridor with proximity to major employers

Tri-Valley Market Snapshot

The Tri-Valley remains one of the most competitive real estate markets in the Bay Area. Demand consistently outpaces supply, particularly for single-family homes in top school districts. Experienced representation is crucial.

  • Median home prices range from $950K–$1.6M+
  • Multiple-offer situations remain common
  • Dublin among the fastest-growing cities in CA
  • Julie brings sharp Bay Area market strategy

About Julie

Dedicated to the people behind every transaction

CEO & Owner, Christensen Real Estate Group

Julie is a 4th generation real estate professional — and yes, she briefly tried to escape the family business. Before finding her way back to real estate, Julie spent the early part of her career in Los Angeles, fully immersed in the fast-paced world of entertainment. Her first role was with Simon Cowell's company as an A&R representative, scouting talent for The X-Factor and America's Got Talent. She then went on to serve as a personal assistant to Barbara Streisand and James Brolin, and later as Studio Manager at a recording studio in Venice Beach. Not a bad résumé.

But real estate runs deep in her DNA. In May of 2016, Julie returned to her roots and earned her license — hitting the ground running and never looking back. In her very first year, she earned the prestigious Rookie of the Year award, a testament to the drive and people skills she sharpened in Hollywood. She has since become a consistently top-performing agent within Keller Williams, adding accolades to her name year after year.

Today, as CEO & Owner of Christensen Real Estate Group, Julie brings that same energy and genuine care to every client. She thrives on getting excited with her clients — celebrating new beginnings, big milestones, and the unique joy of finding the right home. Helping people navigate what is often the largest financial transaction of their lives isn't just her job — it's one of her greatest privileges.
